Classic mid-1980s New York old-school hip-hop, 104 BPM, straight 4/4. Two commanding male MCs trade short lines in clipped, percussive speech-rap, then shout the HALL HOUSE hook together. Bold, playful, deadpan confidence; clear British English architectural vocabulary. Sparse hard drum-machine groove: booming kick, cracking snare on 2 and 4, dry handclaps, tight eighth-note hats, occasional open hat. Lean syncopated bass, short gritty rock-guitar stabs, DJ scratches and stop-start breakbeats. Strong downbeat attacks, internal rhymes, emphatic last words, brief unison doubles. Leave space between bars; no constant double-time. Dry upfront voices, raw sampler grit, small room ambience. Hook is chanted, never melodically sung. Drop the beat for "Oh, behave!" and "Very private", then slam back in. No trap rolls, Auto-Tune, lush pads, choir or church reverb. Finish with a final unison HALL HOUSE, scratch and hard stop.
